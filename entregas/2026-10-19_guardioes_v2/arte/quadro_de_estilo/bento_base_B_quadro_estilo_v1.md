# Quadro de estilo — Velho Bento / Tipo B

## Identificação

- **Guardião:** Velho Bento
- **Base:** Cabana do Caçador
- **Tipo:** B — base isométrica
- **Intenção:** estabelecer a linguagem visual comum das bases: Mata Atlântica rural, suspense psicológico, leitura clara de tabuleiro e contraste entre luar frio e presença humana âmbar.
- **Imagem final:** `bento_base_B_quadro_estilo_v1.png`

- **Ferramenta:** gerador de imagens integrado, seguido por uma edição dirigida da projeção da câmera.

## Prompt inicial exato

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square game environment concept art, style-board approval image, base type B
Primary request: Create the isometric base of Velho Bento, the Hunter's Cabin, for the game "A Última Noite".

Scene/backdrop: Rural Brazil in the mid-20th century, a dense Atlantic Forest clearing with jequitibá trunks, embaúbas, giant ferns, bromeliads, tangled vines, bamboo, wet fallen leaves, and muted red clay ground. The entire clearing and building must be visible with generous ground margin around it.

Subject: A low hunter's cabin built from dark rough planks and logs, with a low thatched roof and a rustic stone chimney releasing a thin thread of smoke. Around it: animal pelts drying on a line with no blood, hanging snare traps, a stacked firewood pile, and a chopping stump with an axe embedded in it. All five gameplay points of interest must be clearly visible at once: a side window with warm amber light shining through shutter slats; a distinct back door opening toward the forest; a distinct front door with one wooden step; fresh human footprints pressed into wet red mud leading to the front door; a dense bush a few meters from the cabin that can function as a hiding place. No people or animals visible.

Style/medium: Stylized painterly digital illustration, visible brush strokes, simplified forms, strong readable silhouettes, slightly rough graphic edges, muted dark palette, polished environment concept art. Finish inspiration only: dramatic hand-painted game concept art, never imitating a specific franchise. Not photorealistic, not glossy 3D, not cute cartoon, not anime, not pixel art.

Composition/framing: Strict isometric dimetric 2:1 view, camera approximately 30 degrees elevation and rotated 45 degrees. Parallel ground axes at approximately 26.5 degrees, no converging lines, no perspective distortion, no wide-angle lens, no vanishing point. Square 1:1 composition. Whole cabin and all five points of interest fit comfortably in frame. Doors, window, cabin silhouette, footprints, and hiding bush remain recognizable when reduced to a 200-pixel thumbnail.

Lighting/mood: Full moon is the main light, entering exclusively from the upper-left, cold pale blue-green, casting long consistent shadows toward the lower-right. Warm amber kerosene-lamp or hearth light spills from the window and door gaps, clearly signaling occupancy. Dark psychological suspense, subtle unease, supernatural only suggested through atmosphere and slightly unsettling shadow placement; nothing supernatural shown. Dark but highly legible.

Color palette: deep night blue #0E1A2B; dark moss green #1F3328; pale moonlight #9FB8C8; muted red earth #5A3A2E; gray-brown wood #4A3F36; amber lamp light #E89A3C; warm fire glow #F2C26B. Accent color rust red #8C3B2A used sparingly on no more than 10 percent of the image.

Materials/textures: damp red clay, wet leaves, weathered timber, rough thatch, moss, worn iron traps, matte natural surfaces.

Constraints: This must read as rural Brazilian Atlantic Forest, not European fantasy. Show exactly one building and no characters. All required points of interest must be visually distinct. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no UI, no labels.

Avoid: perspective camera, cinematic lens distortion, medieval European architecture, castle, cathedral stonework, armor, Nordic pine forest, giant glowing mushrooms, visible magic, glowing eyes, monsters, gore, explicit blood, heavy fog hiding the structure, near-black illegibility, photorealism, shiny 3D rendering.
```

Use case: stylized-concept

Edit only the camera projection and scene layout geometry of the supplied image. Preserve the same rural Brazilian hunter's cabin, Atlantic Forest vegetation, all props, palette, materials, cold moonlight from upper-left, warm amber light, footprints, front door, back door, side window, hiding bush, pelts, traps, firewood, stump and axe.

Required correction: convert the composition to a strict orthographic isometric dimetric 2:1 game-board view. Camera approximately 30 degrees elevation, rotated 45 degrees. Both horizontal ground axes must remain parallel across the entire image and run at approximately +26.5 and -26.5 degrees. No converging lines, no perspective shrinking with distance, no wide-angle or cinematic lens. Make the cabin read like a coherent game-board tile while keeping natural painterly depth through overlap, lighting and value rather than perspective distortion. Keep the whole cabin centered with generous clearing visible around every side. All five gameplay points must remain simultaneously distinct and readable at 200-pixel thumbnail size.

Maintain stylized painterly digital illustration with visible brush strokes and strong silhouettes. Keep the scene dark but legible. No people, animals, text, letters, numbers, signs, logos, watermark, frame, UI, labels, gore, blood, magic, monsters, medieval European fantasy, photorealism, glossy 3D, anime, cute cartoon or pixel art. Do not add or remove any gameplay point of interest.

## Critério mais fraco

**Exatidão geométrica da projeção dimétrica 2:1.** A correção aproximou a composição de uma peça de tabuleiro e reduziu a sensação de lente, mas a pintura orgânica ainda não oferece a precisão matemática de um desenho técnico ortográfico. Esse é o principal ponto a aprovar ou pedir ajuste antes de fixar o ângulo para as demais bases.
